About us

"As the tree,
so the fruit."

Our competence is based on over 35 years of professional experience in business development — especially in IT and display technologies — and on a career shaped by working across the cultures of the world.

The journey

1962

Roots in Northern Germany

Born in Mecklenburg-Vorpommern. Graduated from high school in Halle/Saale with university qualification. During his studies and PhD, Lutz Moehr experienced eight years of Romania first-hand. In October 1989 he broke off his PhD and escaped — via Hungary — to Western Germany.

1991–1998

SONY Europe

Various management positions within SONY Europe, including more than 20 business trips to Japan in eight years — building deep familiarity with international markets, Asian business culture and the display-technology supply chain.

1999–2000

ELSA AG

Management roles at ELSA AG, combining an engineering background with broad practical experience across the whole product life cycle: from conception through production, support, marketing and sales, up to profit-and-loss responsibility.

2001

DNS Consult founded

DNS Consult was founded as an independent IT consulting company — later establishing 3D-CC, the 3D-Competence-Center, as its specialized division for human senses based perception in 3D: Seeing, Hearing, Haptics.

Today

Gold Partner of 3IT Berlin

3D-CC is a Gold Partner of 3IT Berlin, the Innovation Center for Immersive Imaging Technologies operated by the Fraunhofer Heinrich Hertz Institute — alongside partners such as Fraunhofer IIS, Sky, ARRI and Deutsche Telekom.

Independence

No trading. No own products.

3D-CC does not sell hardware or software and has no products of its own. That independence is the foundation of every recommendation: the only interest we represent is yours.

Approach

We bring the system together.

Individual 3D components are only as good as the system they form. Our focus is the practical integration of stand-alone hardware and software into complete systems that work.
