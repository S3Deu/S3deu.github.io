01 · Priority one

Consulting & Networking

Consulting and networking come first — technology second. We advise and accompany; we do not assemble systems ourselves, with the sole exception of demo setups in the S3D®-Showroom. Built on 35+ years in the IT and display-technology business, our real asset is a wide network of manufacturers, content providers, sales channels and research institutions. We connect organisations with the right potential cooperation partners — as an independent networker, not a vendor — so that nobody has to reinvent the wheel and everyone can concentrate on what they do best.

  • Independent, vendor-neutral advice — no trading, no own products
  • Introductions to the right cooperation partners from a 35-year network
  • Support for both directions: sourcing partners, or being introduced as one
  • Requirements and budget analysis, tailored to your defined budget
02 · Framework

S3D®-Discovery-Program

A structured programme — developed and refined since 2013 — that guides organisations through the full landscape of Stereo-3D: the fundamentals of human 3D perception, the taxonomy of available visualization technologies and hardware, and the concrete application areas for your industry. The Discovery-Program turns a confusing market of stand-alone components into a clear, budget-conscious adoption path.

  • Fundamentals of human senses based perception in 3D — Seeing, Hearing, Haptics
  • Complete overview of S3D® visualization technologies and systems
  • Application mapping for your specific industry and use case
  • Vendor-independent evaluation and adoption roadmap
03 · Experience

S3D®-Showroom

Seeing is believing. The S3D®-Showroom is a permanent exhibition concept with a professional VIP consulting service: current 3D visualization technologies and Stereo-3D enabled software applications, experienced live and compared directly — with a clear focus on practical use and integration into complete systems.

  • Live experience of 3D visualization technologies and applications
  • Direct comparison of products and technologies
  • Independent, neutral consultation by qualified personnel
  • Individual presentations in a calm atmosphere — flexible visits, twelve months a year, free of charge
